Aislely

User Guide

Everything the app does, and how to get at it.

Aislely sorts your shopping list into the aisle order of whichever store you're standing in, so you shop start to finish in one pass. Everything runs on your device: no account, no server, no tracking, and it works with no signal.

The six tabs

Tab What it's for
Plan Build the list: add items, set quantities, choose which stores they belong to
Shop Check items off in aisle order, watch the running total, finish the trip
Layout Set this store's aisle order, colour, currency, and loyalty card
Cats Manage categories (the aisles themselves)
When Your shopping day and calendar reminders
Spend Where your money goes (Pro)

Above the tabs is the store row. Tapping a store does two things at once: it filters the list to the items meant for that store, and reorders everything into that store's aisle layout. Aislely reopens on whichever store you last picked.

Getting started

  1. Rename the sample stores on the Layout tab to your real ones ("Shoprite", "Costco"), and add any others with + Add store.
  2. On Layout, drag the categories into the order you actually walk that store. Do this once per store — it's what makes the whole app work.
  3. Start adding items on Plan.

Your currency is picked from your phone's region automatically. You don't need to set anything up for it.

Building your list (Plan)

Type an item and press Add. Aislely suggests a category as you type; change it with the dropdown if the guess is wrong.

Quantities are free-form text — "2", "1 lb", "1 dozen". Adding an item that's already on the list offers to combine the quantities instead of creating a duplicate.

Send to decides which stores an item belongs to. It starts on the store you have selected, so items land where you're shopping without extra taps. Tap any store to add or remove it, or All stores to send it everywhere. After each add it resets to the selected store, so one unusual item never changes the rest.

The star marks an item as a staple — something you buy routinely. Staples always carry over when you finish a trip.

If you've bought something before, a line under the box tells you when you last bought it and what you paid.

Adding by voice

Tap the microphone beside the add box and speak. Everything is shown for review before it's saved — nothing happens behind your back. Tap the ? next to the mic for the full list of phrases.

Say What happens
"Add milk" / "Add eggs, milk, and bread" Adds one or several items
"Add two gallons of milk" Adds milk with quantity "2 gallons"
"Add milk and eggs to Costco" Adds them to that store's list
"Set milk to 3" Changes a quantity
"Remove milk" Removes it, after you confirm
"Remove milk from Costco" Takes it off that store, keeps it on your others
"Clear my Costco list" Clears that store, with an Undo afterwards

Tips: separate items with commas, say the store last, and add names containing "and" (like "mac and cheese") on their own so they don't get split.

Shopping (Shop)

The Shop tab is a stripped-down view for the aisle: items in your store's order, one tap to check each off. Aislely nudges you if you're about to leave an aisle with something still on it.

Checking an item off is always one tap and never requires a price.

Money: the running total and currency

The running total

At the top of Shop you'll see something like:

≈ $84.20
9 confirmed, 3 estimated, 2 unpriced

Its job is to tell you roughly where you stand — "am I at $60 or $160" — before you reach the register.

  • Estimated items are priced from what you last paid at that store. This works from your first trip onward; you don't have to enter anything.
  • Confirmed items are ones you priced or confirmed on this trip.
  • Unpriced items have no price yet and add nothing to the total.

Tap the total to see every item and where its number came from. The ≈ disappears only when every item has been confirmed today.

Entering prices

Beside each item is its price:

  • Tap it to confirm the estimate is right.
  • Press and hold to type the real price.

The keypad works like a card terminal — type 4 9 9 for $4.99, with no decimal point to hunt for. In a currency with no decimals (yen, won), 499 simply means 499.

Things not on your list

Use the quick-add box at the top of Shop: type the name, optionally the price, and it goes straight into the cart as bought. Impulse buys are exactly what makes a running total drift, so this keeps it honest.

Currency

Aislely uses your phone's region to pick a currency, and every amount it saves remembers which currency it was in — so your history can never be silently rewritten.

To change it: Layout → Currency, then tap the amount shown. Search by code, name, or symbol (EUR, euro, €). If you already have trips recorded, Aislely offers to relabel them — it corrects the label only, and never converts amounts or uses exchange rates.

Shopping in more than one currency? Once a second currency is in play, each store can have its own.

Finishing a trip

Tap Finish trip in Shop.

  1. If the store has a loyalty card, the Checkout screen appears first — card at the top for the start of the transaction, total below for the end.
  2. The total is pre-filled from your running total. Type over it with the receipt figure if you like, or leave it blank — the trip still logs.
  3. If you left anything unchecked, Aislely asks what to do: keep them for next time (out of stock) or clear them too (changed your mind).
  4. Pro users can enter per-item prices for the spend dashboard.

Then the list resets itself:

  • Items you bought are removed — you have them now.
  • Staples always stay, ready for next time.
  • Skipped items follow whichever choice you made.

Loyalty cards and checkout

(Pro) Keep a store's loyalty card in the app instead of your wallet.

  1. Layout → Add loyalty card, then scan the barcode on the physical card. The camera captures both the number and its barcode type, which is what makes it scan correctly at the register.
  2. If scanning won't work, choose Enter manually, type the number, and pick the barcode picture that matches your card.
  3. Check the review screen against the real card, then save.

Show card on the Layout tab displays it any time — useful for an unplanned trip. The screen stays awake while it's open.

Your card never leaves the device: it's excluded from shared lists and exports.

Purchase history

Every item you buy is recorded on your device. On Shop, tap Search history:

  • Search an item to see when you last bought it, at which store, what you paid, and every past purchase of it.
  • Minor typos are fine — "bannana" still finds bananas.
  • To browse instead, use Jump to… for any past month, or Show more for older trips.

Nothing is ever archived or deleted.

Stores and aisle order (Layout)

  • Rename or delete a store at the top. Deleting asks first, and items pinned only to that store go back to all stores — nothing is lost.
  • Aisle order: drag a category by its handle, or use the arrows. Saved per store.
  • Categories you remove from a store still appear on your list under Other.
  • Store colour tints the background so a glance tells you which list you're on.

Reorder the store row itself by long-pressing a store and dragging.

Categories, schedule, spend

  • Cats — add, rename, recolour, or remove categories. Removing one moves its items to "Other"; they're never deleted.
  • When — set your shopping day, then export a calendar reminder. It's a standard .ics file handled by your own calendar app; there's no push server.
  • Spend (Pro) — totals, spend by category, most-bought items, and CSV export.

Sharing a list

Plan → Share list creates a file you send however you like. The other person taps Import and picks it.

The list keeps its store grouping: stores they already have are matched by name, and any they don't have are offered for them to create. Declining one just skips those items. Loyalty cards are never included.

Free vs Pro

Free — aisle-order sorting, unlimited stores, per-item store scope, shopping mode, the running total and price entry, trip totals, purchase-history search, voice, list sharing, and calendar reminders.

Pro (one payment, no subscription) — scan-to-add, the spend dashboard, price history and comparison, CSV export, recipes, templates, staples, item photos, loyalty cards, learned categories, and restock predictions.

Searching your own purchase history is free and always will be — it's your data.

Privacy

Everything lives on your device. There's no account and no server holding your information, so there is nothing to leak, sell, or subpoena.

Camera and microphone are used only when you choose to scan, photograph, or dictate, and they're processed on the device. Two optional features touch the network: importing a recipe from a link, and voice dictation (your phone's own speech service does the transcription). Everything else works offline.

Troubleshooting

An item vanished from my list. It's probably pinned to a different store. Switch stores, or look for the "N items are only on other stores' lists" note.

The running total looks wrong. Check the line under it — if most items are estimated, it's working from old prices. Confirm a few and it'll sharpen up.

The mic button isn't there. Voice needs a browser or device that supports speech input; where it isn't available the button is hidden rather than broken.

A recipe link won't import. Not every site publishes recipe data. Paste the ingredients instead — that always works and needs no connection.

My card says its barcode type isn't supported. A few older symbologies can't be drawn accurately, and showing a wrong barcode at a register is worse than showing none. Use the physical card at that store.

I need to start over. Clearing the app's data removes everything, permanently — there's no cloud copy. Export a CSV or share your list first if you want a record.
